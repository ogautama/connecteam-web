Public site · /tools/disc

The DISC test, rebuilt around the person who sent the link

Since PR #41 this page is unlisted: nobody browses to it, they arrive on a member's ?ref= link, on a phone, from WhatsApp. The page still reads like a page you found on a website — a marketing hero that never leaves, a result laid out like a blog post, and a form asking a stranger for their number on behalf of nobody in particular. This redesign makes the referrer visible, gives each of the three screens the whole viewport, and ends on one action instead of three.

What's wrong

Everything below was reproduced from the running page at 375 × 812. The last one is the reason for the redesign; the rest are what makes it worth doing at the same time.

The hero never leaves The intro block is in page.tsx, above the client component — so it sits over all 24 questions and over the result. It eats 480 of 812 mobile pixels, permanently.
Question 1 is below the fold On a 375 px screen not one of the four options is visible on arrival. Every question needs a scroll, 24 times.
The page moves under your thumb Statements vary in length, so answering reflows the page — measured at up to 80 px of shift. The next tap target isn't where the last one was.
The progress bar lies at both ends Width is step / 24: it reads 0 % while you answer question 1, and the last answer jumps straight to the result at 96 %. It never fills. aria-valuenow is off by one too.
Going back costs you the answer "← Sebelumnya" walks back, but there's no way forward except re-answering. Checking what you picked means picking again.
Nothing is remembered A refresh, a phone call, an accidental back-swipe — 24 answers gone, silently, with no way to resume.
The header has zero slack at 375 px Measured: 24 + 139 + 111 + 77 + 24 = 375 exactly, so justify-between has nothing left to distribute and the wordmark, nav and Login button sit flush — "CONNECTeamHome" reads as one word. At 320 px "Join Us" wraps to two lines and the Login pill runs past the right padding. (MarketingLayout, not DISC-specific — but this is where it's seen.)
The result reads like a blog post A left-aligned 672 px column under a stale hero still advertising "24 pertanyaan · ~2 menit". The profile name is set no larger than a section heading.
Nothing marks the dominant traits Four bars, four percentages, equal weight. The two traits that actually produced "Sang Penata" are not distinguished from the two that didn't.
Three endings compete Navy "Simpan hasil saya", then red "Gabung Sekarang", then "Ulangi tes" — stacked, in three different visual weights. There is no identifiable primary action.
The ask comes after the payoff The full result is already on screen when the form appears. Nothing is withheld, and nothing is offered — so there's no reason left to type a phone number.
The referrer is invisible ?ref= is read on line 20 and used for lead ownership — and never shown. A member sends this link personally; the page greets their prospect as an anonymous visitor and asks for a WhatsApp number with no name attached to the request.

Decisions

All five settled 2026-08-07 — each landed on the recommendation. Recorded here as the input to the plan.

Naming the referrer means resolving ?ref= for display

Today resolveRecruiter() runs server-side inside the action and returns an id. Showing "Olivia" needs the name resolved in page.tsx and passed down — a public page turning an invite code into a member's first name. Anyone can guess codes and enumerate names. Decided: first name only, never the full name, email or photo; unknown codes fall back to the brand silently rather than erroring.

Withhold the result until the number is given?

The standard funnel move, and it would raise lead volume. It also makes the tool feel like bait, which is a poor first impression from someone the visitor knows personally. Decided: keep everything free, as it is today. The ask is earned by naming Olivia, not by a paywall.

The share card needs rendering machinery

Either client-side canvas (no new route, awkward font loading) or a dynamic OG-image route (clean, but the result would have to be encoded in the URL — which puts a personality result in a shareable link). Decided: ship the redesign without it, and treat the share card as its own plan.

The header's zero slack is not this plan's

MarketingLayout has no responsive treatment at all — one flex row, justify-between, no breakpoint. It fits at 375 px with 0 px to spare and breaks below that, and it affects / and /join equally. Decided: separate fix, but do it first — it's the first thing a referred prospect sees.

Percentages don't always sum to 100

score.ts rounds each trait independently, so a result can read 21 + 13 + 33 + 34. Invisible in four separate bars; visible in a single stacked one. Decided: keep the rounding, give the spectrum's last segment the remainder so the bar always closes.

What this does not change

The instrument

All 24 questions, their shuffled statement order, the tally in score.ts, BLEND_MARGIN, and all ten profile keys stay exactly as they are. This is a redesign of the surface, not of the test.

Attribution and scoping

saveDiscLead() keeps re-scoring server-side, keeps ignoring a client-supplied result, and keeps resolving ownership from ?ref= — a signed-in member still owns their own result regardless of the link they arrived on. /member/leads is untouched.

Visibility

The route stays public and unlisted. DISC_LISTED stays false, the nav link and home-page teaser stay gone, and the member onboarding link stays.

The copy

Every profile's summary, strengths, atWork and watchOut string is used verbatim. Only the page's own framing copy — hero, helper lines, the ask, the confirmation — is rewritten.
