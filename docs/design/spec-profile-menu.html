Design reference — navigation, amends Plan 07c

Onboarding checklist drops to 4: "Isi Data" becomes a "Profile" menu item, two license/class steps hidden

Two separate calls made 2026-08-05, both landing on the same checklist, following Plan 07c (which built the /join → Isi Data linking neither of these touches). The Isi Data page itself, its form, and its data are unchanged — only where a member finds it changes. Static comparison, same convention as spec-quest-hub.html: click the toggle below to switch, click the account button to open the dropdown.

💡 Change 1 — Isi Data moves to the account menu. The entry point goes into the account dropdown (top-right — same spot as "Member Space" and "Log out"), not a new sidebar item. It's labeled "Profile" there, even though the page it opens still reads "Isi Data" (the page/heading itself is out of scope). The row is removed from the checklist entirely — it no longer counts toward Onboarding's total.
🙈 Change 2 — two steps hidden. Lisensi AAJI & AASI and Kelas MFC & Sertifikasi Produk come off the checklist too. Both are "Segera hadir" placeholders today with no real content behind them, and as of 2026-08-05 no member has ever checked either one off — there are no OnboardingProgress rows for lisensi-aaji-aasi or kelas-mfc-sertifikasi — so hiding them costs nobody any progress. Note this isn't "hide every placeholder": Download PruForce and Setup WA, IG are placeholders too and stay.
⚠️ Side effect worth knowing about: together the two changes take the checklist from 7 items to 4, so the progress percentage's denominator changes for everyone. Only Change 1 costs anyone visible credit: a member who'd already checked off Isi Data loses it, since the checklist won't have that row to show a checkmark on anymore, even though their underlying OnboardingProgress row is untouched in the database. Worth deciding whether those join-isi-data rows get deleted or left orphaned.
