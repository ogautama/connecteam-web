DISC leads, scoped to your downline

Plan 16 mockup. Every DISC submission gets an owner in the recruitment tree — resolved from the member's referral link (/tools/disc?ref=<inviteCode>), falling back to the root leader — and /member/leads shows exactly the viewer's subtree: an agent sees their own referrals, a leader their downline, root everything. Members' own auto-saved results ride along wearing an Anggota badge.
